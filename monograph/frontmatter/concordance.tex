A reference of the form ``[Ch.~$n$, Thm.~$a.b$]'' points to Theorem $n.a.b$ of this book; the papers' own section-wise numbering is preserved inside each chapter. References to the applied notes (Phys~1, Holo~1, Net~1, Ctrl~1) are by the labels below; the biological notes Bio~1--11 form Volume~II, \emph{Algebraic Genomics}, and are not chapters of this volume.
